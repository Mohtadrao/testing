ClearPath combines specialized manufacturing capabilities with EPC and facility management services for industrial and project requirements. Clearpath Engineering & Services Pvt Ltd focuses on manufacturing services such as CNC machining, mold making, fabrication, and prototyping. Separately, ClearPath’s EPC operations provide design and engineering, construction management, equipment and fleet, skilled human capital, and facility management services.
